## Design Context

### Users

People managing a personal recipe collection on Android phones, often while meal planning or cooking in bright kitchen conditions with divided attention or messy hands. Their core job is to save, organize, find, compare, and open recipes quickly while retaining ownership of offline-first data.

### Brand Personality

Calm, tactile, and quietly authoritative. My RecipeBible should feel like a trusted culinary journal: warm and personal, but practical rather than ornamental or whimsical.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the established Culinary Editorial language on participating surfaces: parchment and tonal paper layers, Playfair Display hierarchy, Plus Jakarta Sans utility text, restrained paprika actions, and sparing sage and amber semantics. Prefer compact, readable layouts and friendly rounded rectangles. Support light, dark, and OLED themes. Avoid visual clutter, gratuitous effects, unsupported social concepts, and motion that calls attention to itself.

### Design Principles

- Make finding and opening the right recipe fast under limited attention.
- Preserve user-owned data, existing navigation, and real product behavior while improving presentation.
- Build hierarchy with typography and tonal surfaces before adding decoration or elevation.
- Keep controls readable, reachable, and at least 48 dp across phone and tablet layouts.
- Use brief, purposeful, transform-and-opacity motion; honor reduced-motion settings.
